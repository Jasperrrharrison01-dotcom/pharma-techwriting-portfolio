# PROTOCOL-PHARM-2026-002: In Vivo Evaluation of Moringa oleifera Against Doxorubicin-Induced Nephrotoxicity

## 1. Study Overview & Objective
This study protocol defines the experimental framework to evaluate the renoprotective and therapeutic potential of *Moringa oleifera* leaf extract (MO) against acute nephrotoxicity induced by Doxorubicin (DOX) in an *in vivo* rodent model.
* **Primary Therapeutic Target:** Mitigation of chemotherapy-induced renal parenchymal injury.
* **Mechanism of Action Under Test:** Downregulation of oxidative stress, inhibition of pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-α, IL-6), and suppression of apoptotic executioner markers (Caspase-3).
---
## 2. Experimental Design & Animal Grouping
### 2.1 Animal Model Specifications
* **Subject Species:** Female Rats (*Rattus norvegicus*)
* **Sample Size ($N$):** 30 subjects total ($n = 5$ per experimental group)
* **Acclimatization:** Minimum 7 days under standard laboratory conditions (temperature $22 \pm 3^\circ\text{C}$, 12-hour light/dark cycle) prior to dosing.
### 2.2 Dosing Architecture (Prophylactic Design)
Treatments are administered orally ($p.o.$) for seven (7) consecutive days. On Day 8, a single dose of Doxorubicin ($15\text{ mg/kg } i.p.$ in normal saline) is administered to induction groups to trigger acute renal toxicity.

| Group ID | Treatment Classification | Daily Oral Pre-Treatment (Days 1–7) | Single Induction Dose (Day 8) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Group I** | Normal Control | Distilled Water ($p.o.$) | Vehicle Control |
| **Group II** | Negative Control (DOX) | Distilled Water ($p.o.$) | Doxorubicin ($15\text{ mg/kg } i.p.$) |
| **Group III** | Low-Dose Test | MO Extract ($100\text{ mg/kg } p.o.$) | Doxorubicin ($15\text{ mg/kg } i.p.$) |
| **Group IV** | Mid-Dose Test | MO Extract ($200\text{ mg/kg } p.o.$) | Doxorubicin ($15\text{ mg/kg } i.p.$) |
| **Group V** | High-Dose Test | MO Extract ($400\text{ mg/kg } p.o.$) | Doxorubicin ($15\text{ mg/kg } i.p.$) |
| **Group VI** | Positive Control | Ascorbic Acid / Vit C ($200\text{ mg/kg } p.o.$) | Doxorubicin ($15\text{ mg/kg } i.p.$) |

---
## 3. Sequential Procedure & Euthanasia
1. **Administration Period:** Administer oral treatments every 24 hours at designated dosage volumes for 7 consecutive days.
2. **Induction Step:** On Day 8, administer $15\text{ mg/kg}$ Doxorubicin intraperitoneally ($i.p.$) to Groups II through VI.
3. **Observation & Termination:** 
   * Monitor subjects for 24 hours post-DOX injection.
   * At hour 24, sacrifice all subjects under terminal anesthesia in accordance with humane endpoint guidelines.
4. **Sample Harvesting:**
   * **Blood Capture:** Collect whole blood via cardiac puncture into standard serum separator tubes.
   * **Renal Harvesting:** Excise both kidneys immediately; clean in ice-cold saline ($0.9\%\text{ NaCl}$).
   * **Tissue Processing:** Homogenize left kidney parenchyma for biochemical assays; fix right kidney cross-sections (cortex and medulla) in $10\%$ neutral buffered formalin for histopathology.
---
## 4. Analytical Endpoints & Biomarkers

| Parameter Category | Target Biomarkers Evaluated |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Serum Renal Function Markers** | Serum Creatinine, Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN), Uric Acid, Total Protein, Bilirubin |
| **Parenchymal Oxidative Stress** | Malondialdehyde (MDA), Superoxide Dismutase (SOD), Catalase (CAT), Glutathione Peroxidase (GPx) |
| **Inflammatory & Apoptotic Profile** | TNF-α, Interleukin-6 (IL-6), Caspase-3 Expression |
| **Histopathological Evaluation** | Glomerular Atrophy, Tubular Necrosis, Inflammatory Cell Infiltration |
---

## 5. Statistical Analysis

* **Data Expression:** All results shall be presented as $\text{Mean} \pm \text{S.E.M.}$ (Standard Error of the Mean).
* **Comparative Testing:** Differences across groups shall be analyzed using One-Way Analysis of Variance (ANOVA) followed by Student's $t$-test for pair-wise comparison.
* **Statistical Significance Threshold:** Values where $p < 0.05$ shall be classified as statistically significant.
