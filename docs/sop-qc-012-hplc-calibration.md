## 1. Purpose
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) details the operational steps required to perform routine calibration and system suitability testing on High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) units equipped with UV/Vis detectors prior to quantitative batch assay analysis.
## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all analytical chemists operating HPLC systems (Models HC-500 series) within Quality Control Laboratories 1 through 4.
## 3. Safety & Environmental Controls
### 3.1 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
*   **Eye Protection:** Safety glasses with side shields (ANSI Z87.1 approved).
*   **Hand Protection:** Chemical-resistant nitrile gloves (minimum 4 mil thickness).
*   **Body Protection:** Standard laboratory coat, fully buttoned.
### 3.2 Chemical Hazards
*   **Methanol (HPLC Grade):** Highly flammable liquid and vapor. Toxic if swallowed or inhaled. Handle exclusively under an operational fume hood or connected exhaust system.

## 4. Materials & Equipment

| Item Category | Item Description | Minimum Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Equipment** | Isocratic/Gradient HPLC System | UV/Vis Detector (190–600 nm range) |
| **Reagents** | Methanol (Mobile Phase A) | HPLC Grade (≥ 99.9% purity) |
| **Reagents** | Purified Water (Mobile Phase B) | Milli-Q Grade (18.2 MΩ·cm at 25°C) |
| **Reference Standard** | Caffeine Reference Standard | USP Grade (Lot RS-2026-A) |

---
## 5. Sequential Operating Procedure
### 5.1 System Startup & Degassing
1. **Inspect Reservoirs:** Verify that Mobile Phase A (Methanol) and Mobile Phase B (Milli-Q Water) contain a minimum volume of 500 mL.
2. **Purge System:** Open the prime purge valve and flush all pump channels at `5.0 mL/min` for **3 minutes** to remove trapped air bubbles.
3. **Equilibrate Column:** Close the purge valve. Set the flow rate to `1.0 mL/min` (60:40 Methanol:Water) and purge the C18 analytical column for **15 minutes** until baseline stabilization is achieved.
### 5.2 System Suitability Injection
1. **Prepare Standard Solution:** Inject `10 µL` of USP Caffeine Reference Standard (`0.01 mg/mL` concentration) onto the column.
2. **Execute Run:** Initiate the chromatographic run under the following parameters:
   *   **Flow Rate:** `1.0 mL/min`
   *   **Detection Wavelength:** `272 nm`
   *   **Column Temperature:** `25°C (± 1°C)`
   *   **Run Duration:** `8.0 minutes`
3. **Repeat:** Perform five ($5$) consecutive replicate injections of the caffeine standard solution.
